## 1. Define

The question: 
Did the customer counts at their flagship downtown Seattle location dropped the last six months correlate with the other 31 cafes?

The decision this will inform: 
If confirmed → The client should move forward closing the Seattle location.
If ruled out → I suggest change with management.

What "done" looks like: 
Metric: Percentage of customers that stopped purchasing from the Seattle location.
Comparison: January customer count vs. the average of June and July customer counts.
Threshold: Confirmed if over the last six months if customer count drop was greater than 15% at the Seattle location.
